Privacy

The Bootstrap CSS Breakpoint Indicator extension does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

What the extension does

When you turn the indicator on, the extension adds a small stylesheet to the page in the current tab so the active Bootstrap breakpoint is shown in the corner of the window. When you open the extension's popup, it also checks the stylesheet links on the current page to work out which Bootstrap version the page uses. That check happens entirely in your browser, and nothing about the page is recorded or sent anywhere.

Data stored on your device

The extension saves your settings (whether the indicator is on, the Bootstrap version, and whether to differentiate breakpoints with color) using the browser's local extension storage. These settings stay on your device and are never sent anywhere.

Permissions

  • activeTab and scripting: used only to add and remove the indicator stylesheet, and to detect the Bootstrap version, on the page you are viewing.
  • storage: used only to remember your settings.

No tracking

The extension has no analytics, advertising, or third-party services, and it makes no network requests.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, please open an issue on GitHub.
